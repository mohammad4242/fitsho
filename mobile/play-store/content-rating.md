# Fitician content-rating basis

Status: **draft for the IARC questionnaire**

Use the submitted production build to complete the Play Console content-rating
questionnaire. The current product basis is:

- General fitness and nutrition utility.
- No advertising SDK or paid gambling feature.
- No public user-generated feed or direct user-to-user messaging.
- No sexual content, graphic violence, weapons, or controlled-substance
  promotion.
- Health and nutrition guidance is framed as wellness support and specialist
  workflow content, not as entertainment or medical treatment claims.
- Account creation and sign-in are available to the users allowed by the
  approved product policy; age targeting must be confirmed in Play Console.

Record the final IARC result with the release candidate, and repeat the
questionnaire if the product, audience, or content changes.
