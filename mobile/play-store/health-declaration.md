# Fitician Health apps declaration basis

Status: **prepared for Play Console review; not submitted**

The mobile product provides health and fitness features. In the Health apps
declaration, review and select the categories that match the production
feature set:

- **Activity and Fitness** — workout routines, exercise history, body
  measurements, and body-progress records.
- **Nutrition and Weight Management** — dietary goals, meal planning,
  nutrition tracking, and weight-management support.
- **Healthcare Services and Management** — only if the physician/member
  workflow is enabled in the submitted build and is available to users.
- **Clinical Decision Support** — product and regulatory owners must make the
  final determination for the authorized physician review workflow before
  submission.

The app does not connect to Health Connect, Google Fit, wearable sensors, or a
medical device. It does not claim to diagnose disease, prescribe treatment, or
replace a clinician. It stores user-provided health, fitness, nutrition, and
clinical workflow data according to the approved privacy and deletion policy.

If the submitted production build changes these capabilities, the declaration
must be re-reviewed before upload. The app is not declared here to be a
regulated medical device; legal and regulatory owners remain responsible for
that determination in each target market.

Reference: [Health apps declaration form guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14738291).
